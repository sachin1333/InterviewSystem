# What the candidate sees before they start

This is a plain-language surface. It is not a legalistic T&Cs dump. Keep it readable.

## Who you are talking to

You will be interviewed by a simulated business stakeholder. It is powered by an AI model. Another AI model will review your work afterward. A human hiring manager will see the results and make the final call.

## What we record

- Everything you type or submit in the notebook and chat.
- Your code and its outputs.
- A timeline of when you answered and paused.

We do **not** record video or audio unless you explicitly enable it.

## What we'd like to read (opt-in, per source)

Tick only what you're OK with.

- [ ] **Resume** (upload a PDF or docx)
- [ ] **LinkedIn** (paste your profile URL and your About + Experience text)
- [ ] **Personal blog or site** (paste the URL)
- [ ] **GitHub handle** (public data only)

Anything you don't tick is not fetched. You can start the interview without ticking anything.

## What we strip before anyone — human or AI — sees it

- Your name, email, phone, address.
- Your photo.
- The names of people you worked with.
- Anything that looks like a demographic signal (age, gender, nationality).

These are kept in a separate, recruiter-only record so the company can contact you. The interviewer agent never sees them.

## How long we keep it

- Full session recording: 30 days after the interview.
- Raw documents you uploaded: 30 days after the interview.
- Scoring summary (structured, no PII beyond what's needed for audit): kept with your application.

## Your rights

- You can stop the interview at any time. The partial recording is deleted on request.
- You can request a copy of everything we stored about you.
- You can ask for a human re-review of any score you disagree with.

## The big things

- The AI is a screening tool. A human makes the final decision.
- Model scores can be wrong, especially on communication and nuance. The system flags low-confidence scores for human review.
