# Question Primitives — Challenger Reference

## think_aloud
Force candidate to externalize first-five-steps reasoning out loud.
- **Duration**: 90–120 seconds
- **Primary Signals**: problem_framing, insight_interp
- **When to pick**: Early in the interview to establish baseline problem comprehension and mental model.

## socratic_rebuttal
Challenge a stated method choice; require defense or pivot.
- **Duration**: 60–90 seconds
- **Primary Signals**: model_rationale, communication
- **When to pick**: After candidate proposes an approach; validates depth of reasoning.

## counterfactual
Inject 'assume X changed' to disrupt cached reasoning.
- **Duration**: 45–90 seconds
- **Primary Signals**: model_rationale, problem_framing
- **When to pick**: Mid-interview to test flexibility and adaptive thinking.

## resume_deep_dive
Ask for class imbalance ratios / dataset shapes from a stated prior project.
- **Duration**: 60–120 seconds
- **Primary Signals**: problem_framing
- **When to pick**: When candidate references prior work; validates concrete project knowledge.

## verbal_whiteboard
Describe a multi-stage pipeline (e.g., retraining loop) in words only.
- **Duration**: 120–180 seconds
- **Primary Signals**: experiment_design, communication
- **When to pick**: Late in interview to assess systems-thinking and ability to articulate complex flows.

## one_bullet
One sentence: why does this metric matter?
- **Duration**: 15–30 seconds
- **Primary Signals**: communication
- **When to pick**: Final probe; validates ability to distill to leadership-ready explanation.

---

## Cheating-Defense Primitives

These primitives are designed to resist memorized or rehearsed answers:
- **socratic_rebuttal** — Requires real-time defense of claimed choices
- **counterfactual** — Disrupts cached reasoning with assumption shifts
- **resume_deep_dive** — Spot-checks concrete project details
